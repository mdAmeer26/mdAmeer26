# 💫 About Me:
I’m an Electronics & Communication Engineering graduate building at the intersection of software, AI, data, and real-world problems.<br><br>I’m driven by the space between “what if?” and “let’s build it.” I enjoy taking ownership, learning what I don’t know, turning ideas into working products, and leaving things better than I found them.<br><br>Beyond technology, I enjoy bringing people together, leading from the front, helping others grow, and creating an environment where people feel motivated and happy to contribute.<br><br>I’m not trying to know everything.<br><br>I’m trying to become someone who can learn anything worth knowing—and build something meaningful with it.<br>
