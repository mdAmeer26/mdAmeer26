# 💫 About Me:
I’m interested in the space between “this should be possible” and “let’s build it.”<br><br>That space has shaped the way I learn, work, and think.<br><br>I’m an Electronics & Communication Engineering graduate who gradually found myself drawn beyond the classroom and into software, AI, data, and building things that solve real problems. I’ve worked across different kinds of projects, explored unfamiliar technologies, and learned that the most interesting part of engineering is rarely knowing the answer—it’s knowing how to find it.<br><br>I build with curiosity, but I work with ownership.<br><br>When something needs to move forward, I like stepping in, figuring it out, bringing people together, and seeing it through. I’ve had the opportunity to lead teams, coordinate initiatives, teach people starting from zero, collaborate with different kinds of people, and learn just as much from others as I’ve taught.<br><br>And I care about something that doesn’t show up in a tech stack:<br><br>the people.<br><br>I want the people I work with to feel comfortable asking questions, excited to contribute, and better about themselves after working together. I believe good work can be demanding without making the people around it miserable—and that the best teams are built on trust, ownership, curiosity, and a little bit of joy.<br><br>I don’t try to present myself as someone who already knows everything.<br><br>I’m much more interested in being the person who can enter an unfamiliar problem, learn what matters, build what is needed, take responsibility for the outcome, and keep improving until the work is worth putting my name on.<br><br>That mindset has taken me from engineering and research to AI applications, full-stack products, data-driven systems, automation, and collaborative projects.<br><br>The technologies will change.<br>The problems will change.<br>The tools will change.<br><br>I want to remain the kind of engineer who keeps learning, keeps building, and keeps moving forward.<br><br>Because for me, engineering isn't just about writing code.<br><br>It’s about turning curiosity into creation,<br>taking ownership when it matters,<br>bringing people with you,<br>and leaving something better than you found it.<br><br>Curiosity starts it.<br>Ownership carries it.<br>People give it meaning.<br>Building makes it real.<br>
